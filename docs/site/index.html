Momentum support

A companion for tracking health, best-of-three matches and a 50-minute round timer.

Need help or want to report a bug? Email momentumuvs@gmail.com. Include your app version, phone model, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or signing keys.

Starting a match

Choose names and starting health, then select who goes first or roll the D20s. The round timer starts after the first-player choice. Tap a player’s name for the attack calculator.

Results and corrections

Use Match to record a win or draw and correct earlier results. Undo reverses the latest health change or manual game result. Next game keeps the round timer and record. Reset → New match clears both.

Timer and battery

The timer keeps elapsed time while the app is closed and catches up when reopened. Pause it when your event allows. Help contains optional vibration alerts and Keep screen awake. Turn off Keep screen awake to allow normal screen sleep. Timer alerts are not lock-screen alarms; follow your organizer’s official clock.

Saved data

Game state and settings are saved on this device. Removing app data may remove saved games. Read the privacy policy for details.
